Southeast Housing Market Analysis

A comparison of Southeast metro areas using housing costs, population growth and employment data from the Census Bureau, FHFA and BLS.

Python Housing Census Labor market Regional analysis

Business impact

Helps a reader distinguish a low-cost market from one where population and employment are also growing.

2019–2023Population growth comparison

Business question

Which Southeast metros combine attainable housing costs with growth and a resilient labor market?

Method

Compare each metro against its peers

The pipeline combines Census housing, income and population estimates with FHFA house price data and BLS labor-market measures. It ranks inputs across metros and combines them into affordability, growth and labor-market scores. The overall score weights the three dimensions at 33%, 34% and 33%.

A second score takes the geometric mean of growth and labor-market strength. A metro that scores poorly on either measure falls in that ranking, even when housing is inexpensive.

Results

Huntsville leads on both measures

Huntsville, Alabama, ranks first on both scores in the project snapshot. Fayetteville–Springdale–Rogers and Nashville follow on the balance score. Florence–Muscle Shoals and Decatur lead on affordability but have weaker growth signals.

Scope

These rankings compare the metros and data vintages in the notebook. The separate coursework housing models analyze individual properties.
